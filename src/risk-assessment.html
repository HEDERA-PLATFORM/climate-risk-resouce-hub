Assessment phases

The guide will walk institutions through these phases. Detailed content, tool links, and dataset recommendations will be added at each step.

01

Scope & perimeter

Define the portfolio, time horizons, and climate scenarios to assess. Select relevant physical and transition hazards.

02

Exposure mapping

Geolocate assets or counterparties. Overlay with climate hazard layers from the datasets catalogue.

03

Sensitivity & vulnerability

Assess how exposed entities respond to climate hazards. Apply sector-specific vulnerability models.

04

Risk quantification

Translate physical and transition exposures into financial metrics: PD shifts, collateral value at risk, EAD adjustments.

05

Scenario analysis

Run forward-looking scenarios (NGFS, IPCC SSPs) to understand how risk evolves under different warming pathways.

06

Disclosure & reporting

Structure outputs for TCFD, CSRD, or supervisory reporting. Templates and worked examples included.
