Climate datasets navigator

Climate and climate-risk data sources

Catalogue of publicly available datasets, tools, and data providersrelated to climate and climate-related events (temperature, drought, water stress, wildfire and more).
The goal is to provide microfinance stakeholders with available information to understand climate risks and to identify which aspects are more relevant in selected contexts. Each dataset contains a description of relevant variables and format, availabily, and key facts about its usage in the context of climate-risk assessment.
